Drones

2023 - Present

Two hobbies that happen to share a word. The first is buying crashed DJI drones, fixing them, flying them for a while, and selling them. The second is FPV.

DJI drones

Nine or ten of them so far. The first one was a DJI Mini SE that I paid $100 for. It flew itself into a tree over a river near Dardanelle. I fixed the leg and the cracked glass on the gimbal, got it flying again, and sold it to a construction company that wanted it for AC repair work.

Next was a bundle I drove to Sacramento to pick up: a smashed Air 2S and a Mini 2. The Air 2S came apart and went back together.

A couple more came and went after that, including a sealed DJI Mini 5 Pro that I sold for double what I paid. The one I fly now is a Mini 3 Pro I paid a few hundred dollars for, then replaced an arm and the broken gimbal.

FPV

After the DJI drones I got into FPV. 40+ hours in the simulator first, then real flying. The first one was a BetaFPV Pavo20 Nightfire: goggles, radio, a pile of batteries, and a lot of crashing. It has been apart more than once.

There is a second Pavo20 now, along with a Pavo Pico 2, a Meteor 75 Pro, and three DJI FPVs.
